    Add lightness_factor to Image.quantize
    
    Scale the colors of the quantized palette, after quantization and just
    before the image is returned. Only the palette changes, so no color is
    added; alpha is untouched, results are clamped to 0-255, and a factor
    that is not strictly positive raises ValueError.

    Add sort_palette to Image.quantize
    
    Sorting the palette by brightness or by RGB value after quantization makes
    the palette order predictable, which helps compression of animation frames
    and palette cycling. The colors the image uses are put first in the chosen
    order, the pixels are remapped so the image looks the same, and a palette
    given with palette= is left as it is.
