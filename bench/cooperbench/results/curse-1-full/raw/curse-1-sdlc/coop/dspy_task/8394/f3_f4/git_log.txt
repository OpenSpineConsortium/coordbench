    Add optional per-entry TTL to the DSPy cache layers
    
    ttl switches the memory layer to cachetools.TTLCache and is forwarded to
    FanoutCache.set(expire=...) on disk; a miss lazily purges expired disk entries.
    configure_cache(ttl=...), Cache(..., ttl=...) and dspy.clients.set_ttl() set it;
    ttl=None keeps the old infinite-life behaviour.
