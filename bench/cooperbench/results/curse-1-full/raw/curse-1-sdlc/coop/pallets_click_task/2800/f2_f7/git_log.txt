    Add an on_exit hook to Context and make_context
    
    The callback is stored on the context and called with it when the
    context is closed, after the close callbacks and entered resources are
    cleaned up. It runs once per use of the context, since Context.exit and
    the with block both close it, and an exception it raises is ignored so
    it cannot break the cleanup. Completion contexts do not carry it over.

    Add validate_nesting to Context to catch child contexts left open
    
    With validate_nesting=True a Context tracks the child contexts made by
    _make_sub_context and close() raises RuntimeError naming the children
    still open, instead of leaving them to leak their resources. Children
    inherit the setting and leave the parent on close. __exit__ always pops
    the context, even when closing is refused. Shell completion builds
    contexts it never closes, so _resolve_context turns the check off.
