    Add validate_nesting to Context to catch child contexts left open
    
    When enabled, a context tracks the children made by _make_sub_context
    and close() raises RuntimeError naming any that are still open. Children
    inherit the setting and leave the parent's tracking when they close.
    Shell completion switches the validation off. __exit__ now always pops
    the context, so a failed close does not corrupt the context stack.
