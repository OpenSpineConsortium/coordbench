    Add Context.save_state and restore_state, and use them in shell completion
    
    save_state takes a snapshot of every attribute of a context, deep copying
    params, obj, _meta, default_map and _parameter_source so nested changes
    do not reach it. restore_state sets the attributes back as copies, leaves
    the with depth and the exit stack alone so no resource is orphaned, and
    refills _meta in place since it is shared with the parent. Shell
    completion snapshots the resolved context and restores it afterwards.
