    Add escape_shell to click.edit()
    
    With escape_shell=True the filename and the words of the editor command are
    quoted with shlex.quote before they reach the shell, so characters such as
    ;, | or a backtick are not run as commands. It defaults to False, which
    keeps the command as it was.
