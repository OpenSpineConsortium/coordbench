    Add path_transform to template loaders
    
    An optional function that rewrites a template name before the loader
    resolves it, for FileSystemLoader, DictLoader, FunctionLoader,
    PackageLoader, PrefixLoader, ChoiceLoader and ModuleLoader. The two
    composite loaders transform once at the top and hand the result to their
    children. TemplateNotFound keeps the name that was asked for, and errors
    from the function propagate. Without the argument nothing changes.
