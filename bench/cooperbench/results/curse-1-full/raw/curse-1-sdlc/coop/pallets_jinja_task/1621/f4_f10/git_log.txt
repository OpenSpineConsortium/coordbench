    Add template path aliasing to loaders
    
    BaseLoader gains add_alias and remove_alias. Every loader resolves an alias
    to its target, once and without chaining, before it looks the template up,
    so TemplateNotFound names the target. A template loaded under an alias goes
    stale in the environment cache when the alias is removed or changed.
