Relative speed

Hatched bars are runs whose times are not a full log's own. The OpenHands SDK files' step times are interpolated (spaced evenly over each agent's phase). A run with no transcript (the session driver's pilots) has only the hub's record of its coordination calls: its steps count those calls alone and are marked ">=", and its steps per minute, time per step, communication share, longest idle gap and times to the first edit and test run are not measured. Hatched runs are left out of the ranks unless included. ">=" marks a lower bound, with its reason in the value's title.

Failure modes

Each cell: present, absent, not labelled or not published, with its source: judge (judge.json, a model's label), rule (judge.json, a symptom level App G's rule decided with no model, or set over the judge's), human (cause labels), heuristic (computed here) or data (the outcome). CooperBench released no communication-error or symptom labels, so its runs show heuristics and the 23 cause labels only. A failure present is red and absent green; role division, resource division and negotiation are coordination behaviours (Sec 6.4), not failures, so present is purple and absent plain.
